Stage toolbar redesign

The Seams & inliers, New panorama and Download PNG controls in the output stage head. Today they sit in a 2 × 2 grid that doubles the head's height and puts the one action people came for at the bottom of the block. This mock puts all three on one row: the view toggle, then Download, then a quiet reset at the far edge.

  • One row, 56px headToggle │ download, reset. When the stage is too narrow, the toolbar moves below the chips as one unit and never splits.
  • Download comes first after the dividerThe main action sits right after the view toggle. The + reset is at the far edge, small and quiet, so it doesn't compete with Download.
  • The toggle looks like a switchA small track shows on or off in both states. When it is on, the button keeps the spec's accent-soft fill.
  • Reset becomes an iconNew panorama is the third reset on screen. The rail already has "Clear all" and "+ Start new panorama", so here it is a quiet 32px icon with a tooltip.

04 Alternative — Option B · the view switch moves onto the image

The overlay control becomes a segmented Image / Seams & inliers switch in the viewport's top-left corner, next to the thing it changes. The head keeps only actions, so it fits on one row down to about 820px. This needs a change to spec §3.1, which moved the toggle out of the plate on purpose (see the comment on ResultPlate).
